What you are looking at. The ship — its position, velocity, tilt, clamps, speed modes and its two option pods — is computed live by the port, one logic frame per 16.896 ms, from the board's own arithmetic. That code compares 0 divergent frames against the board over 2,200 logic frames on 34 columns (pgm.py flyaround). Everything else in the picture is a replayed board capture: the port does not build the display list — main-loop call #4 $23D2AE is unported, and so are 18 of the 20 top-level object handlers — so the background, the enemies and the HUD come from 161 captured frames of the fly-around scenario and loop. The renderer drawing all of it is the port's own JS, and it is pixel-identical to MAME on 136 frame pairs, 13,647,872 of 13,647,872 pixels (pgm.py pixslice). There are no weapons: wave 5 came back BLOCKED, no enemy or shot handler is translated, and the fire keys reach a named throw rather than a bullet.
